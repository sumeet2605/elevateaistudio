# Caption fonts

- Noto Sans Bold is from [Noto Fonts](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans/NotoSans-Bold.ttf).
- Noto Sans Devanagari Bold is from [Noto Fonts](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSansDevanagari/NotoSansDevanagari-Bold.ttf).

The fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. The license notices are included in this directory.

Regular weights from the same upstream families are included for the Cinematic Minimal preset and caption weight control: [Latin](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans/NotoSans-Regular.ttf) and [Devanagari](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSansDevanagari/NotoSansDevanagari-Regular.ttf).
